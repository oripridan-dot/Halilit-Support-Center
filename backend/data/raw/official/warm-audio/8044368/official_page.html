WA-CX24

Large-Diaphragm Stereo Tube Condenser Microphone

Faithful Recreation Of The Most Legendary Stereo Tube Condenser Microphone Of All Time

MSRP $1599

Potent Pairing

WA273-EQDual-Channel British Mic Preamplifier + EQ

  1. Bringing Back the Most Unattainable Vintage Stereo Mic

    The WA-CX24 is a faithful recreation of the most iconic stereo tube condenser mic ever made. Designed to capture the rich, three-dimensional sound behind countless classic recordings, it delivers the same wide stereo image and natural warmth that made the original a studio staple. With dual large-diaphragm capsules and true tube circuitry, the WA-CX24 reproduces the smooth detail that made it a favorite on orchestras, pianos, and drum overheads. Famous for sessions with artists and engineers like Elvis Costello and Steve Albini, the original’s sound profile & iconic multi-pattern stereo functionality is accurately preserved with the WA-CX24.

  2. True Stereo Sound, No-Compromise Components

    In addition to the original sound profile, WA-CX24 recreates the critical dual 180-degree rotating capsules for exceptional flexibility to achieve precise stereo imaging in audio recordings. Independently adjusting the position of each capsule allows for error-proof stereo pickup patterns for various recording techniques, such as XY, Blumlein, mid-side, or a custom angle tailored to the sound source and recording environment. Built around dual all-brass CK12-style capsules & two TAB Funkenwerk (AMI) US-made output transformers, the WA-CX24 delivers professional stereo recordings with phase-coherent vocals, intricate piano performances, and epic stereo room sounds.

What you need to know

Like the original C24-style mic, the WA-CX24 includes all the features & functionally that deliver a complete and professional recording experience.

Rotate the top capsule up to a full 180 degrees independently from the bottom capsule to achieve error-proof XY, Blumlein, and various stereo imaging options. Mix and match polar patterns for different blends of focus & space. 

The WA-CX24 has three key polar patterns on both capsules - Cardioid, Omni, & Figure-8. Six additional patterns in-between provide ultimate flexibility and versatility for a variety of stereo recording applications. Achieve phase-choherent Blumlein and mid-side recordings with just one mic.

The WA-CX24 uses a premium 12AY7 vacuum tube to complement the CK12-style capsule for added warmth and clarity without harsh high end.

The two custom USA-made AMI  transformers drive the full natural sound captured by the capsule delivering that renowned Austrian sound.
